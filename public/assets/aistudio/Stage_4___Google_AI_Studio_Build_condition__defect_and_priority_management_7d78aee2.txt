Extend the application with Infrastructure Condition Assessment, Defect Management and Maintenance Prioritisation.

Allow users to record inspections and defects against individual assets.

Include:
- Condition ratings: Good, Fair, Poor and Critical.
- Defect type, description, severity, location and photographs.
- Inspection date, inspector and supporting notes.
- Recommended maintenance action.
- Maintenance priority and target completion date.
- Defect status: Open, Assigned, In Progress, Resolved and Verified.

Implement a transparent, configurable priority scoring model using condition, defect severity, safety risk, infrastructure importance and service disruption. Display the factors contributing to each priority.

Allow authorised users to review and override recommendations with a recorded reason.

Clearly distinguish demonstration recommendations from verified engineering assessments. Do not present automated scores as certified structural safety decisions.
