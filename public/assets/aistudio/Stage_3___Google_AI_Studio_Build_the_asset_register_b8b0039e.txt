Extend the application with a complete Infrastructure Asset Register.

Create records for roads, bridges, culverts and drainage assets.

Each asset must support:
- Unique Asset ID.
- Asset type and name.
- Province, district, LLG and location.
- Latitude and longitude.
- Description and technical characteristics.
- Condition classification and condition score.
- Defects and risk classification.
- Maintenance priority and recommended action.
- Last inspection and next inspection date.
- Photographs, supporting documents and maintenance history.

Provide searchable, sortable asset tables and asset creation, viewing, editing and archiving workflows. Validate required fields and prevent duplicate asset IDs.

Use a reusable data model and clearly labelled demonstration records. Prepare the application for persistent database storage; do not claim that data is permanently saved until a database is connected and tested.
